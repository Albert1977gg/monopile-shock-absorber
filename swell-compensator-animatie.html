Key equations, uncertainty, and model scope

Mc ẍ₁ + cc ẋ₁ + (kc + kw)x₁ − kwx₂ = kcz + ccżM ẍ₂ + cdẋ₂ + kwx₂ − kwx₁ = 0

Vessel dynamics: the vessel motion is imposed as z(t); vessel mass and heave dynamics are not modeled.

Stiffness used in every live calculation: kw = 0.350 MN/m, the effective baseline value in the paper’s Table B.1.

Uncalibrated parameter: draghead damping cd = 500 kN·s/m.

Future work identified in the paper: nonlinear gas pressure, line losses, stroke and pressure limits, seabed contact, and experimental validation.

How it works

The accumulator and cylinder body are fixed to the vessel and move with the deck. The piston rod and cable sheave move relative to the cylinder body, absorbing part of the vessel motion.

Mass and model limits

The dynamic simulation uses M = 4,000 kg for submerged load plus added mass; 23,000 kg is the static draghead mass. Vessel heave is prescribed as an input, so vessel dynamics are not modeled. Seabed contact and friction are also excluded; the seabed here is a visual reference.

Illustrative animation, not a scale drawing or live design calculation. Motion is slowed and visually enlarged. Seabed contact and friction are not included in the dynamic model. Actual performance depends on mass, stiffness, damping, stroke, and wave period.
